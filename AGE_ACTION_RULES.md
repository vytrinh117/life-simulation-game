## v7.1 household activity rules
- Infant (0–1): no independent reading, journaling, TV, computer, phone or stove use. Use caregiver story time, sensory play, radio/music and communication activities.
- Toddler (2–4): no independent reading/journal/computer/phone. Toys, picture books with caregiver, simple art and radio are available. TV is short caregiver-approved screen time.
- Young child (5–7): early reading appears; picture journaling begins around 6. TV/shared computer/stove access uses caregiver permission.
- Child/teen minors: household electronics, owned electronic devices and stove/cooking appliances use a caregiver-permission check. Permission is remembered for that device class for the current in-game day.
- Phone still requires both minimum phone age and actual ownership before caregiver permission can be requested for use.
- Radio music requires no screen permission. Radio news appears only when age/communication suggests the character can understand it.
