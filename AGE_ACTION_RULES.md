# Age & Eligibility Rules — v7

The visible action should normally change with age rather than showing an adult action that only fails after clicking. Logic validation also exists underneath the UI.

| Stage | Ages | Examples of independent play | Caregiver/permission-dependent | Hidden / restricted |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Infant | 0–1 | react, play, sleep | feeding, bathing, toileting, dressing, outings | phone, clubs, jobs, independent purchases/travel/romance |
| Toddler | 2–4 | play, emerging communication | food, potty learning, bathing, dressing, kindergarten decision, outings | phone, jobs, independent travel/business |
| Young child | 5–7 | school/play, simple chores, basic self-care as learned | purchases, small selling from 6, outings, trips | phone use, regular jobs, investing, dating |
| Child | 8–12 | school, homework, clubs, hobbies, chores, small-business ideas | expensive purchases, travel, yard sales/stands | independent phone use, jobs, adult romance/investing |
| Early teen | 13–15 | school, friendships, age-appropriate crushes, stronger self-care | travel/expensive purchases/household rules | dating apps, investing, fully independent travel |
| Teen | 16–17 | phone if owned, part-time jobs, social apps, more planning | travel/major purchases still depend on household context | investing, adult dating app, fully independent travel |
| Young adult | 18–22 | independent travel/work/purchases, adult phone apps, investing | contextual only | — |
| Adult | 23–39 | full adult life systems | contextual only | — |
| Middle age | 40–59 | career/family/travel/health/finance | contextual only | — |
| Older adult | 60+ | retirement, relationships, travel, health, legacy-oriented play | contextual only | — |

Central rules include phone age, work age, adult/investing/dating-app age, independent travel, small-business start, cooking-help age and driving-age baseline. Location-specific laws are not yet simulated as a complete legal database.
